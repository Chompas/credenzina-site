Credenzina

Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 24, 2026

Credenzina is a personal album-tracking app. It does not have accounts, does not run ads, and does not use analytics or tracking of any kind. This policy explains what little the app does touch.

What Credenzina stores

Everything you save (albums, ratings, notes, and the artists you follow) is stored on your device. If you're signed in to iCloud, it's also synced to your own private iCloud database so it appears on your other devices and survives reinstalling the app. That database belongs to your Apple Account: Credenzina's developer has no server and can't see or access anything in it. You can turn syncing off in Settings › [your name] › iCloud. If you use the in-app export feature, the file it creates goes wherever you choose to send it, and Credenzina doesn't keep a copy or see where it went.

What leaves your device, and why

  • Searching for an album sends your search text (an artist or album name) to Apple's public iTunes Search API and to the MusicBrainz API. Neither request includes any information that identifies you personally, just the words you typed.
  • Pasting a link (an Apple Music or Spotify album URL) makes the app fetch that page's public metadata to work out which album you meant. No account or API key is involved, and nothing about you is sent beyond the link itself.
  • Album artwork is downloaded from Apple's servers and cached on your device so covers don't have to be downloaded again.
  • Scanning a screenshot uses Apple's on-device Vision framework to read text from an image you choose. The image is never uploaded. Recognition happens entirely on your device, and only the album names it finds are used to run the searches described above.
  • Noticing what you play is optional and off until you turn it on. When it's on, Credenzina asks Apple Music for your recently played albums through Apple's MusicKit and compares them with your queue on your device. Only the album identifiers needed for the next comparison are kept, and nothing from your listening history is sent anywhere else.
  • Last.fm scrobbling is optional and off until you connect your Last.fm account. You sign in on Last.fm's own page, so Credenzina never sees your password; it keeps only the access key Last.fm returns, in your device's Keychain. When you mark an album as listened, its track names, the album and artist, and the times they were played are sent to Last.fm to add to your profile. Last.fm's own privacy policy covers what happens there. Disconnecting removes the key from your device.
  • Playing an album hands off to the Apple Music or Spotify app you choose. What happens after that is between you and that app.

What Credenzina does not do

  • No Credenzina accounts, sign-in, or personal profile.
  • No analytics, crash reporting, or usage tracking.
  • No advertising or ad networks.
  • No sharing of your data with anyone, apart from the Last.fm scrobbles you choose to turn on.

Children's privacy

Credenzina does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children, because it does not collect personal information from anyone.
