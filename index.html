Credenzina

A place for the albums you mean to hear.

Save a record when you think of it. When you have time, open it in Apple Music or Spotify and listen to the whole thing. No recommendations, no feed, no account.

Support

Found a bug, or have a question? Email chompas+credenzina@gmail.com. Mentioning your iPhone model and iOS version helps.

Common questions

  • Where is my data? Only on your iPhone. There is no account and no sync. Use Export My List in the options menu (⋯) to keep a backup.
  • Why can't I find an album? Search uses Apple's music catalog, so records that aren't on Apple Music may not show up. Pasting an Apple Music or Spotify link into the search field usually finds it.
  • Why does it ask how an album was? After you open an album in Apple Music or Spotify, Credenzina waits about as long as the record runs, then offers to rate it. You can dismiss it.
